Why Thermal Break 20?

Built to Defeat Chicagoland Winters

Standard aluminum conducts cold directly into the home. ATA RABAH's **Thermal Break 20** system separates the interior and exterior aluminum profiles with a high-strength, glass-fiber reinforced polyamide barrier.

Zero Interior Condensation Prevents frost and moisture buildup on interior frames even during sub-zero Midwest freezes.
Acoustic Dampening Multi-chamber profile geometry paired with insulated glass units drastically cuts street and wind noise.
Zero Warping or Rotting Unlike wood or vinyl, architectural-grade aluminum maintains razor-sharp alignment for decades with zero maintenance.
